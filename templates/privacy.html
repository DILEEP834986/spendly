Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you register, we collect your name, email address, and a password (stored in hashed form). When you use Spendly, we also collect the expense details you choose to enter, such as amount, category, date, and description. We do not collect payment card or bank account information.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data only to run Spendly for you: to sign you in, to store and display your expenses, and to produce the summaries and filters you ask for. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your expense data for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in the Spendly database and is accessible only through your account. We take reasonable steps to protect it, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure. You may ask us to delete your account and its data at any time, and we will remove it from our active systems.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser makes a request to Google's servers when you visit our pages. We do not share your expense data with any third party. If we add other services in the future, such as analytics or email delivery, we will update this policy.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy or want to request access to or deletion of your data, contact us at support@spendly.example.
